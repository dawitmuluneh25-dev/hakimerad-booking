## 7. Go-live checklist

1. Freeze `main`; CI green on the exact SHA; tag the release.
2. Back up the production database and uploads (or confirm a clean first install).
3. Write the production environment file with mode `0600`: `NODE_ENV`, `AUTH_ISSUER_URL`, `AUTH_CLIENT_ID`, `AUTH_COOKIE_SECRET` (fresh, ≥32 chars), `AUTH_REDIRECT_URI`, `DATABASE_PATH`, `DATA_DIR`, plus `AUTH_CLIENT_SECRET` if confidential. `AUTH_DEV_LOGIN` unset.
4. Apply migrations; confirm `schema_version` is current.
5. Deploy the release; restart; confirm exactly **one** process is running.
6. `pnpm smoke https://<host>` → 10/10.
7. Verify headers, TLS, and HTTP→HTTPS redirect on the live host.
8. Sign in as the first administrator, claim the administrator role, create staff accounts and assign roles; verify physician verification.
9. Enter operational settings: contacts, hours, slot step, rooms, equipment, durations, channels.
10. Submit one real referral end to end with a test account; then remove it per the retention policy.
11. Confirm the scheduled backup ran and that a restore works.
12. Announce in writing to the clinic; monitor error rate, queue depth, outbox backlog and disk for 24 hours.
13. Record the rollback command in the launch record (redeploy previous artifact; database untouched).

## 9. Deliberately deferred

- Payment gateway (payment status stays manual: unset / due / paid / waived).
- Analytics dashboards and marketing attribution.
- Clustering or multi-instance operation — SQLite and local uploads require a single instance.
- Antivirus scanning of uploaded documents.
- Native mobile apps.
- Automated clinical reminders beyond Y6's simple scheduler, if B10 defers them.
